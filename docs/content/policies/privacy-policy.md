**The short version.** We collect what we need to sell you cleaning product and deliver it, we don't sell your data, and you can ask us what we hold about you at any time.

**What we collect.** Your name, email, delivery address, phone number and order history when you buy; your email when you join our mailing list; payment details are processed by Shopify Payments / our payment providers and never stored by us; standard analytics data (pages visited, device, rough location) to make the site work better.

**What we use it for.** Fulfilling and delivering orders; managing Subscribe & Save renewals; answering support requests; sending the marketing you've opted into (like the spring reminder — unsubscribe anytime with one click); improving the site.

**Who we share it with.** The services that run the store: Shopify (hosting and checkout), our couriers (delivery details only), our subscription billing app, our email platform, and post-purchase tooling. Each only gets what it needs to do its job. We don't sell or rent your information to anyone.

**How long we keep it.** Order records as long as we're required to for tax and consumer-law purposes; marketing data until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.

**Your rights.** Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it (where the law lets us). Email [support email] and we'll sort it.

**Cookies.** The site uses cookies for the cart, checkout and analytics. Your browser can block them; the cart won't work without the essential ones.

**Contact.** Privacy questions: [support email] · Maximum Wash Ltd, [registered address]. If you're not happy with our response you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner (privacy.org.nz).
