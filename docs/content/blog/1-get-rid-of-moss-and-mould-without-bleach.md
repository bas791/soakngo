## Intro (publish-ready)

Every spring, half of New Zealand walks outside, looks at the fence, and reaches for a bottle of bleach. We get it — bleach is fast, cheap and satisfying for about a fortnight. But if you've ever dealt with the fumes, the careful tarpaulin work, or the mysterious brown patch in the garden afterwards, you already know the trade-off. Here's the thing the professionals figured out years ago: outdoors, you don't need speed. You need persistence. The weather is out there working on your house 365 days a year — the trick is to put it on your side. This guide covers why moss and mould keep coming back, why bleach is a short-term fix, and how the soak-and-walk-away method gets a longer-lasting result without any of the drama.
