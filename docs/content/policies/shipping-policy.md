**Where we ship.** Everywhere in New Zealand — North Island, South Island, and yes, Waiheke. We don't currently ship overseas (the moss over there isn't our problem yet).

**How much it costs.** Orders over **$79** ship **free** NZ-wide. Under that, shipping is calculated at checkout based on your address and order size. The 20L Concentrate ships as an oversized parcel and may carry an additional charge, shown at checkout.

**How long it takes.** We dispatch from our warehouse within 1 working day of your order (orders before 12pm usually go same day). Delivery is typically **2–4 working days** for urban addresses; rural delivery can add 1–2 working days.

**Subscription orders.** Subscribe & Save orders dispatch automatically on your schedule. You'll get an email before each order is processed, and tracking once it ships.

**Tracking.** Every order gets a tracking link by email as soon as it's on the truck.

**Damaged or missing deliveries.** If your order arrives damaged, leaking, or doesn't arrive at all, contact us within 7 days at [support email] with your order number and (for damage) a photo. We'll replace it or refund you — no drama.

**Address errors.** If you've put the wrong address in, contact us as soon as possible. Once a parcel's in the courier network we can't redirect it, but we'll help sort out what we can.
