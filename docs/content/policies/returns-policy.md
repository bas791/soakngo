**The short version.** If Soak n Go doesn't work, we'll make it right. We wash buildings for a living — we know what clean looks like, and we stand behind the product.

**Performance guarantee.** Used as directed (right coverage, dry surface, no rain for 12 hours, and enough time for the weather to work — remember stubborn growth can take a few months), if you're not seeing results, contact us within **6 months** of purchase. Send your order number and a couple of photos of the treated area. We'll talk you through it, and if the product's not doing its job we'll replace it or refund it.

**Change of mind.** Unopened, unused bottles in original condition can be returned within **30 days** of delivery for a refund of the product price. Return shipping for change-of-mind is on you. Opened bottles can't be returned for change of mind (it's a liquid; you understand).

**Damaged or faulty products.** Arrived leaking, damaged or otherwise not right? Contact us within 7 days with a photo and we'll replace or refund, including any shipping.

**Subscriptions.** You can pause, skip or cancel your subscription anytime before the next billing date via the link in any subscription email. Refunds for subscription orders already dispatched follow the same rules as one-off orders.

**How refunds are paid.** To your original payment method, within 5 working days of approval.

**Your legal rights.** Nothing in this policy limits your rights under the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993 or the Fair Trading Act 1986.
