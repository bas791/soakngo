## Intro (publish-ready)

Ask ten neighbours how often you should wash your house and you'll get ten confident, contradictory answers. So here's one from a company that washes buildings for a living: for most New Zealand homes, once a year is the baseline — but the honest answer is "it depends on which way your house faces, what it's made of, and how close you are to the coast or the bush." A shaded south-facing wall in Tauranga grows mould on a completely different schedule to a sun-blasted north wall in Christchurch. The good news: with the right approach, you can stretch the time between proper washes considerably, keep your paint and cladding in better shape, and stay on the right side of your insurance and warranty small print. Here's the full breakdown.
